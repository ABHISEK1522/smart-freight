# 🚚 Smart Freight

### AI-Powered Freight Consolidation & Cold-Chain Risk Prediction

Smart Freight is a smart logistics platform designed to help **farmers, MSMEs, shippers, and freight operators** move goods more efficiently.

The platform analyzes shipments, vehicle capacity, routes, delivery deadlines, costs, and cargo requirements to recommend smarter transportation plans.

Instead of sending multiple partially-filled vehicles on separate journeys, Smart Freight identifies compatible shipments that can be **consolidated into optimized trips**, helping reduce costs, improve vehicle utilization, and minimize unnecessary travel.


## 🎯 The Problem

Small businesses and agricultural producers often face:

- High transportation costs
- Underutilized vehicle capacity
- Multiple unnecessary trips
- Delivery delays
- Traffic and route-related risks
- Cold-chain challenges for perishable goods
- Limited access to intelligent logistics planning

These challenges can lead to higher costs, wasted capacity, and increased risk of cargo spoilage.


## 💡 Our Solution

Smart Freight brings the major logistics decisions together in one platform.

It evaluates:

**Shipments + Vehicle Capacity + Routes + Deadlines + Cost + Risk + Temperature Requirements**

and recommends a more efficient freight plan.

### Example

Instead of:

Shipment A → Truck 1`

Shipment B → Truck 2`

Shipment C → Truck 3`

Smart Freight can identify compatible shipments and recommend:

Shipment A + Shipment B + Shipment C → Optimized Trip

This can help reduce transportation costs while making better use of available vehicle capacity.
